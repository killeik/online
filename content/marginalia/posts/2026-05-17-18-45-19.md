I recently watched [Kung Pow: Enter the Fist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kung_Pow!_Enter_the_Fist). I randomly watched this yt vid [Scientifically Proving The Funniest Movie Of All Time](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K7j9ONAplwY) where he tries to prove that _Kung Pow_ is the funniest movie of all time.

I liked the story about the production of that movie - It is somewhere between vandalism and true art. Steve Oedekerk bought the rights to a random 1976 Hong Kong martial arts film, _Tiger & Crane Fists_, digitally inserted himself into the footage, rewrote the story into absurd nonsense, dubbed himself over most of the characters, and built an entirely new movie from pieces of the original.

It is absolutely a B-movie - maybe even something worse. A bizarre hybrid of cheap 70s martial arts cinema and cheap early-2000s CGI.
Still it was a pretty fun and comfortable movie to watch, laugh and then continue your life  - and that's a good thing. 7/10
